---
name: Product scope
description: User's standing scope and account requirements for Habit Circle.
---
Keep the application simple and easy to use. Do not add functions that were not requested. Where unspecified, make reasonable choices, including design.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this after being asked to choose an authentication approach.

**How to apply:** Preserve the requested My habits, Community, habit tracking, and discussion scope rather than adding speculative settings, analytics, or other modules.

Accounts must be ready to use straight after sign-up, without requiring email confirmation.

**Why:** The user explicitly required immediate access and reiterated following the original description.

**How to apply:** Do not introduce mandatory email confirmation when changing authentication.
